# Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the company. This page covers the essentials for your first two weeks.

## IT Setup

New employees receive a laptop and access credentials on Day 1. IT Support is available through the internal ticketing system. Standard laptop requests are fulfilled within 2 business days. VPN access is required for all employees working remotely more than two days per week.

## Leave Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, capped at 18 days per year. Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 business days in advance for planned leave. Sick leave does not require advance notice but must be reported to your manager on the same day.

## Expense Reimbursement

All expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date. Claims above INR 5,000 require manager approval before submission. The Finance department processes approved claims within 10 business days.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require Director-level sign-off and are reviewed quarterly. All remote employees must be reachable during core hours (10 AM to 4 PM local time).

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice a year, in April and October. Self-assessments are due one week before the review cycle opens. Ratings are calibrated across each department by the department head before being finalized.
